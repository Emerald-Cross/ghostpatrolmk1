## patch-night.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production background for a Halloween video game, widescreen 16:9 landscape. The attached illustration is a palette and inked-clipart style reference ONLY. Create a MUCH quieter, simpler dark pumpkin patch at night, with an extremely spacious flat dark midnight-plum center occupying 70% of the frame, suitable for large readable interface text. At the extreme left and right edges: just a few curved black tree silhouettes and low distant pumpkin vines. At the bottom corners: a few small subdued burnt-orange pumpkins. Low horizon, a little muted teal fog only along the ground, distant violet hills. Cartoon ink outlines and limited screenprint texture. Palette near-black #0e1119, deep plum #201c32, muted lavender #6b6285, dusty teal #426967, small copper orange #c47b3f. Very sparse detail, dark matte finish, no bright stars scattered everywhere, no big moon, no characters, no buildings, no interface, no lettering, no borders, no parchment. Keep center mostly empty and dark. High-quality quiet hand-illustrated game background.

## ghosts-trio.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent sprite atlas for a Halloween video game. Image 1 is the hand-inked cartoon style and restrained palette reference. Image 2 is an office gauze ghost reference; reinterpret its draped fabric and black oval eyes as clipart.
